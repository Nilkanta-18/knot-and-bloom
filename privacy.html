Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Knot & Bloom, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you contact us or place an order, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We May Collect

When you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address
  • Your order details
  • Information you provide about a custom order
  • Messages and other information you voluntarily send to us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information only when needed to:

  • Process and confirm orders
  • Arrange delivery
  • Contact you about your order
  • Handle returns or exchanges
  • Discuss and create custom orders
  • Respond to customer questions or requests

3. WhatsApp

Orders and customer communication are handled through WhatsApp. When you contact us through WhatsApp, your information is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent customer information.

We may share necessary information, such as your name, phone number and delivery address, with a delivery or courier service when required to deliver your order.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or legal process.

5. Customer Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Information Retention

We may keep order and communication information for as long as reasonably necessary for order records, customer service, returns and exchanges, accounting, legal requirements, or other legitimate business purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general customers. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to placing or managing an order.

8. External Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. External services used to operate or communicate through the website may have their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Knot & Bloom through WhatsApp using the contact details provided on our website.
