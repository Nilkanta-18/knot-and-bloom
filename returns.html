Returns & Exchange Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Eligible Returns

Returns are accepted only if the product received is defective or the wrong product was delivered.

Customers must contact Knot & Bloom within 2 days of delivery to report an eligible issue.

2. Reporting an Issue

Please contact us through WhatsApp as soon as possible and provide your order details. We may request photographs or other information to verify the issue.

3. Return & Exchange Charges

Applicable return or exchange charges may apply. These charges may be deducted from any applicable refund or may be payable by the customer.

The applicable charges will be communicated to the customer before the return or exchange is processed.

4. Product Condition

Products should be returned in their original condition, except where the product's defect prevents this.

5. Custom Crochet Products

Custom-made crochet products are made according to the customer's requested specifications.

Custom-made products cannot be returned or exchanged, except where required by applicable law.

6. Non-Eligible Reasons

Returns or exchanges are not accepted for change of mind, incorrect choices made by the customer, or normal variations in colour, size, shape or appearance that are part of handmade products.

7. Approval

All return or exchange requests are subject to verification and approval by Knot & Bloom.

8. Refunds & Replacements

Where a return is approved, Knot & Bloom will communicate the available refund or replacement options and the applicable process.

9. Contact Us

For return or exchange requests, please contact Knot & Bloom through WhatsApp using the contact details provided on our website.
