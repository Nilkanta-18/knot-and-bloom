Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: September 2026

1. Delivery Charges

  • Free delivery on orders of ₹999 or more.
  • For orders below ₹999, delivery charges will depend on the customer's location.
  • The applicable delivery charge will be communicated to the customer before the order is confirmed.

2. Delivery Time

Delivery time depends on the customer's location, product availability, preparation time and the courier service used.

Handmade and custom crochet products may require additional preparation time. The expected delivery time will be communicated when the order is confirmed.

3. Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number.

Delays caused by an incorrect or incomplete address may be outside the control of Knot & Bloom.

4. Cash on Delivery

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD), subject to availability in the customer's location.

The customer must pay the confirmed order amount when the order is delivered.

5. Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed because of courier issues, weather, public holidays, high demand, or other circumstances beyond our control.

6. Failed Delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed because the customer is unavailable, provides an incorrect address, or does not accept the COD order, we may contact the customer to arrange the next steps.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about delivery or your order, please contact Knot & Bloom through WhatsApp using the contact details provided on our website.
